# FAQ

## Why can't I see every page in the app?

Your role controls baseline access. Members may also need explicit permissions for specific resources.

## Why am I being sent to onboarding or connect pages?

New workspaces must finish onboarding before normal app usage. Workspaces without an active integration are routed to the connection flow until at least one integration is active.

## Why doesn't a location or order appear yet?

Initial imports and backfills take time. After the first import, AperioBI re-syncs nightly and on Square webhook events, so new sales should appear automatically. If you just connected Square, wait for the backfill to finish and refresh the page.

## Who can reconnect an integration?

Owners and admins can manage integrations. Members usually need to ask an owner or admin for help.

## How do I change my password?

Open **Profile** from your account menu in the sidebar and update your password there. If you cannot sign in, use **Forgot password?** on the login page to receive a reset link.

## Who should I ask for access to a page I cannot see?

Ask whoever invited you, usually an owner or admin in your workspace. They can adjust your role or grant the specific resource permission you need from the **Team** page.

## Where should I go if something still looks wrong?

Start by checking the relevant help topic for setup or reporting steps. If the issue continues, contact the person who manages your workspace.
