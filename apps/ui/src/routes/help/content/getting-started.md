# Getting started

Welcome to **AperioBI**. This workspace pulls your Square sales, labor, and inventory activity into one place so your team can answer day-to-day operating questions without leaving the app.

This page walks you from your first sign-in to your first useful report.

## What AperioBI gives you

- A live **Dashboard** with revenue, order, and location KPIs.
- A **Report Builder** for drag-and-drop sales, labor, and waste analytics with pivots, comparisons, and exports.
- Browsable **Locations**, **Orders**, and **Inventory** pages backed by synced Square data.
- **Team & permission** controls so members only see what they should.

![Dashboard overview](/images/help/getting-started/dashboard.png)

## First-run checklist

1. **Confirm your profile.** Open **Profile** from the user menu in the sidebar footer and review your name and password.
2. **Connect Square.** Open **Connect** in the sidebar and link your Square account. You'll choose how much history to import (30 days up to all-time).
3. **Wait for the initial sync.** AperioBI imports locations first, then orders, payments, refunds, inventory, and catalog. A backfill banner appears while history is loading.
4. **Open the Dashboard.** Once the first sync finishes, the KPI cards and Monthly Sales chart populate.
5. **Build your first report.** Open **Report Builder**, drag a metric and a row dimension onto the canvas, and pick a date range.

## What members should expect

If you were invited as a member rather than an owner or admin, some sidebar items may be hidden until an owner or admin grants you access. That is normal — members only see resources they have explicit permissions for.

If you cannot see a page you need, ask whoever invited you to open **Team → Manage permissions** for your account.
