# Getting started

Welcome to AperioBI. This workspace helps your team bring Square sales data into one place and turn it into reports you can share internally.

## What to do first

1. Complete your business profile if you are prompted during onboarding.
2. Connect at least one Square account from **Connect** in the sidebar.
3. Wait for the initial sync to finish importing locations and historical data.
4. Open the dashboard or report builder to start exploring results.

## What members should expect

If you were invited into an existing workspace, some pages may be hidden until an owner or admin grants you access. That is normal. Members only see the features and data they have permission to use.

## Where to go next

- Open **Connecting Square** if you still need to link your Square account.
- Open **Reports** to learn how to build and save report views.
- Open **Team management** if you need to invite coworkers or adjust roles.
