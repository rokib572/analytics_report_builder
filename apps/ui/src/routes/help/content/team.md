# Team management

Owners and admins can manage who has access to the workspace.

## Roles at a glance

- **Owner**: full workspace access, including user management.
- **Admin**: full account access for day-to-day operations, including managing most users.
- **Member**: access is limited to the specific features and actions granted to them.

## Inviting teammates

1. Open **Team**.
2. Enter the teammate's email, choose **Admin** or **Member**, and send the invite.
3. For members, open their row to assign the specific resource permissions they need after they accept.

## Permission reminders

- Members can be granted access to specific resources without receiving admin rights.
- Owners and admins bypass normal member permission checks.
- If someone cannot see a page they expect, review both their role and any resource permissions.
